How to read this

  • Typical speed error is the mean absolute difference between the forecast wind speed and the 10-minute mean wind measured at the full hour. On average shows whether the forecast was too strong (+) or too weak (−).
  • Direction error only counts stations where both measured and forecast wind were at least 3 m/s; light winds turn freely and their direction means little.
  • Gusts: forecast gust compared with the strongest gust measured during the hour.
  • FMI official forecast is the meteorologists' edited forecast, the one shown on ilmatieteenlaitos.fi and in its boating weather. It is updated about every hour. MEPS is the raw weather model, run every 3 hours. Both are read at the station's location (MEPS from its nearest 2.5 km grid point).
  • At sea: lighthouse, skerry, island and harbour stations at the sea. Lake and land stations are left out; a land station near the shore usually measures less wind than on open water.
  • The coloured sea is estimated from the sea stations' errors: each point of sea gets a weighted average of the nearby stations (nearer counts more, about 30 km reach), so it is smooth between stations. It fades out 35–90 km from the nearest station; further out nothing is known. Red: the forecast promised less wind than came (the risky direction for boaters); green: about right; blue: the forecast was too pessimistic.
